## Start Here

Inventory IQ does not send every email to the account login email. Alert, summary, and report emails go to notification recipients saved in Edit Data.

## Check In This Order

1. Open Edit Data and review Notifications.
2. Confirm the recipient email is active.
3. Confirm the recipient has the right alert, summary, or report option enabled.
4. Check whether quiet hours are blocking delivery until later.
5. Check spam, junk, or quarantine folders.

## Why Reports Are Different

Reports are not sent automatically just because a report page exists. An admin must open the report, choose recipients, and send it. If no recipient is selected, no report email is sent.

## If It Still Does Not Arrive

Record which email address, report or alert type, and approximate time you expected. That information makes support much easier to diagnose.
