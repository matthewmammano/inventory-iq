## Start Here

Inventory IQ does not automatically send alert, summary, or report email to the squad's login email. They only go to notification recipients saved on the Data page.

## Check In This Order

1. Open **Data** > **Notifications** and confirm the recipient's email is listed and active.
2. Confirm that recipient has the specific alert, summary, or report checkbox enabled — see [Choosing Your Alert And Summary Preferences](choosing-alert-preferences).
3. Check whether Quiet Hours is delaying delivery until later rather than blocking it entirely.
4. Check spam, junk, or quarantine folders. If you find one there, see [Marking Inventory IQ Emails As Important, Not Spam](marking-emails-as-not-spam) so it stops happening.

## Why Reports Are Different

Report emails are never sent automatically just because a report page exists. An admin must open the report and choose recipients before anything sends. If no recipient is selected, no report email goes out.

## If It Still Does Not Arrive

Record the recipient email address, the alert/summary/report type, and the approximate time you expected it. That detail makes support able to trace a specific send instead of guessing.
