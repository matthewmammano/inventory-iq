## The Main Idea

Inventory IQ sends emails for two different reasons: automatic notifications and admin-requested reports. Automatic emails are based on saved notification settings. Report emails happen only when an admin sends a report from a report page.

## Alert Emails

Alert emails point out inventory conditions or activity that may need attention. Examples include low stock, predicted low stock, stockouts, stale counts, rare takeouts, unknown barcodes, and scan actions like Count, Restock, Takeout, or Transfer.

An alert does not always mean something is wrong. It means Inventory IQ found something worth reviewing.

## Summary Emails

Summary emails group recent activity into a recap. They can be daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly depending on the recipient settings. These are useful when someone wants the bigger picture instead of one email per event.

## Report Emails

Report emails are sent from pages like Inventory Levels or History. Inventory Levels reports include CSV spreadsheet-style files for counts. History reports include a CSV file for the selected date range and location scope.
