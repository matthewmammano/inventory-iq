## What History Shows

History is the audit trail for inventory changes. It shows what item changed, which action was used, the quantity, source and destination when applicable, time, and whether it was an admin action.

## How To Audit A Question

1. Start with the location where the count looks wrong.
2. Look for recent Count, Restock, Takeout, or Transfer actions.
3. Check whether the action type matches what really happened.
4. Use a date range when you only care about a specific period.

## Print And Email

Print History opens a browser-printable report. Email Report sends a CSV spreadsheet-style attachment for the selected date range and location scope.

## Good Use

Use History when you need to understand why a number changed, not just what the number is now.
