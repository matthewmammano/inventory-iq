## What Bulk Action Is For

Bulk Action is a faster way to update many items at one location. It is best when you are already reviewing a group of items and do not want to scan each one separately.

## Good Times To Use It

1. Counting many shelf quantities during a review.
2. Recording a delivery that includes several items.
3. Updating a selected group of items in one pass.

## How To Keep History Clean

Enter only the rows that actually changed. Blank rows should stay blank. This keeps history focused on real updates instead of filling it with unchanged items.

## When Not To Use It

Do not use Bulk Action when only one item changed and scanning it would be clearer. A single scan often creates easier history for one-off work.
