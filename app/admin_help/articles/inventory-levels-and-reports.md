## What Inventory Levels Shows

Inventory Levels shows what Inventory IQ believes is currently on hand. It breaks counts down by location and storage so admins can see both totals and where stock sits.

## What To Look For

- Items at zero may need immediate review.
- Items below minimum may need restock planning.
- Storage-level counts can show whether stock is in the wrong place.
- Totals help compare actual stock against expected need.

## Report Options

Print Report opens a browser-printable report. Email Report sends CSV spreadsheet-style attachments, with separate inventory count files by location.
