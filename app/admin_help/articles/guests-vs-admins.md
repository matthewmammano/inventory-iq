## Basic Difference

Guests are for normal day-to-day scanning. Admins are for setup, corrections, reports, and broader control, unlocked with the squad's admin PIN.

## Guest Access

Guest scanners follow the permissions set in Guest Permissions (Settings page). A guest may be allowed to take out stock but not correct counts, or allowed to restock but only in certain storage directions. This keeps routine scanning simple and reduces accidental corrections on a shared device.

## Admin Access

Admins can manage items, barcodes, labels, reports, settings, and use Stock Actions without guest permission limits. Admin access is better for correcting problems because it has none of the guest restrictions.

## Practical Rule

Use guest scanning for routine, repeated work. Switch to admin — see [Account Security](account-security-and-access) for how the PIN and its 2-hour session work — whenever setup, a count correction, a barcode question, a report, or a permission needs review.
