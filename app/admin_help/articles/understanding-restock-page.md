## What The Restock Page Is

The Restock page helps admins decide what may need ordering or review. It combines current inventory levels with recent usage estimates.

## How To Read It

- Current stock is what Inventory IQ believes is on hand.
- Minimum quantity is the point where stock should be watched closely.
- Estimated usage comes from recent scan history.
- Projected warnings suggest an item may run low soon.

## The Most Important Rule

Restock numbers are estimates. Review current stock before ordering. A recent missed scan, unusual event, or wrong Count can affect the recommendation.

## What To Do With A Suspicious Estimate

1. Check the current stock.
2. Review recent History.
3. Correct the quantity with Count if needed.
4. Record new stock with Restock when it arrives.
